Typing Insights & Pro Tips

Improve your muscle memory and accelerate your words per minute with these foundational guidelines.

1. Focus on Accuracy First

Speed is a natural byproduct of clean technique. If you hit keys accurately without looking down at your keyboard, your speed will naturally increase over time.

2. Maintain Home Row Posture

Keep your fingers resting on the home row keys (ASDF for the left hand, JKL; for the right hand). This minimizes finger travel distance across the keyboard.

3. Practice Daily in Short Bursts

Consistent 5-to-10 minute daily practice sessions build long-term neuromuscular pathways much faster than infrequent, long marathons.
